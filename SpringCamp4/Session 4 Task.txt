Multimodal Emotion Recognition




1. Objective
Design and train a deep learning model that classifies human emotions using both:
* Audio data (processed visually as spectrograms for CNN input)

* Generated text transcripts (via speech-to-text for NLP modeling)


________________






2. Dataset: RAVDESS Emotional Speech
   * Source: Kaggle - RAVDESS Emotional Speech Audio
   * Description: 1,440 audio clips of 24 actors.
   * Target Labels (8 Emotions): 01 = neutral, 02 = calm, 03 = happy, 04 = sad, 05 = angry, 06 = fearful, 07 = disgust, 08 = surprised.
   * As you build the model, understand the challenges with this dataset as well
________________




3. Project Phases


Phase 1: Data Engineering & Unimodal Pipelines


A. Audio Preprocessing (CNN Input)
   * Load audio using librosa.
   * Other audio specific preprocessing techniques (do the research).
   * Convert raw waveforms into Mel-Spectrograms.




B. Text Generation & Preprocessing (RNN Input)
   * Use Whisper or other speech recognition models to transcribe the clips into text. It might be difficult to understand the working of these but do try:)
   * Tokenization
   * Padding
C. Unimodal Training
   * Audio CNN: Build a custom CNN to classify emotion from spectrograms. Architecture decisions you make should be based on experimentation.
   * Text RNN: Build an Embedding layer followed by an LSTM or GRU to classify emotion from transcripts.


Phase 2: Multimodal Fusion


Option A: Early Fusion
Extract the "bottleneck" feature vectors from both models (the layer just before the final Softmax output). Concatenate these two vectors into one long vector.
   * Architecture: Pass this joint vector through 2–3 new Dense (Fully Connected) layers.
   * Key Advantage: The model can learn correlations between the sound and the words during the training process.
Option B: Late Fusion 
Keep your Audio CNN and Text RNN as two completely separate models. Obtain the probability outputs (Softmax scores) from both.
   1. Averaging: Take the mean of the two probability vectors.
   2. Weighted Averaging: Assign more weight to the Audio model (0.7×Audio+0.3×Text) to see if one modality is more reliable.
   3. Maximum Rule: For each sample, take the prediction from the model that has the highest confidence score.


Phase 3: Evaluation & Analysis
   * Compare the Accuracy of: (1) Audio-only, (2) Text-only, and (3) Multimodal.


________________








4. Technical Implementation Details
Loss Function
   * Categorical Cross-Entropy: Since this is a multi-class classification problem.
   * If the dataset is imbalanced, consider using "Class Weights."
Metrics
   * Accuracy: Overall correctness.
   * Precision/Recall & F1-Score: For understanding which specific emotions are being missed.
   * Confusion Matrix: Heat map to visualize which emotions are being confused (is calm being mistaken for neutral?).
Tools
   * Framework: TensorFlow/Keras or PyTorch.
   * Audio: Librosa, Torchaudio.
   * NLP: Hugging Face, Scikit-learn.
   * Visualization: Matplotlib, Seaborn.
